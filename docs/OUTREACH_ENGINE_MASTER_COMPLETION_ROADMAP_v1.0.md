Status: LOCKED FOR EXECUTION

## 1. Product truth

Outreach Engine is the human and commercial operating network around SecurePay.

SecurePay core remains the authority for identity, agreements, conditions, payment readiness, settlement, fees, Plug attribution and financial entitlement. Outreach may display and explain that truth but must not invent it.

The operating loop is:

LISTEN → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → CREATE → APPROVE → DISTRIBUTE → ENGAGE → MEASURE → LEARN → ACT AGAIN

The governing question remains:

> What should SecurePay do next, and why?

The market-facing line remains:

> Money should follow the agreement.

## 2. Three truths used during implementation

1. Lovable project `d193aa9b-79d9-4e59-b3ee-1eb38690bbc6` is the visual/product reference for Outreach.
2. `kimaniks001/Outreach-Engine` `main` is production implementation truth for Outreach.
3. `kimaniks001/SecurePayAPI` is backend authority truth for identity, market standing, opportunities, agreements and financial entitlement.

Do not redesign the visual system away from the Lovable reference. Preserve its premium restrained command-centre character, generous whitespace, calm green/orange use, intent-first language, Kenyan market realism and Quiet Trust tone.

## 3. Locked architecture

### Staff side

Today → Intelligence → Campaigns → Studio → Approval → Audiences → Distribution → Engagement → Impact → Growth Director

Supporting departments include Marketing, Brand, Compliance, Legal, CX, Product/Tech, Finance, Partnerships, HR/Training and Leadership.

### Market Network side

Community LIVE → My Market → Learn → Opportunities → Market Kit → People → Support

Market Network actors use SecurePay KS identity. Training capability, Plug enrollment, Master authority, Community authority, referral provenance and financial entitlement are separate authorities.

### Community principle

> Money stays quiet. People come alive here.

Community LIVE is the public town square. Circles are private belonging. My Market is the private commercial desk.

## 4. AI Studio principle

Studio must not be built around one AI model.

A Model Registry describes approved models/providers by capability, allowed use, cost/risk class and current availability. The Studio can route a job to one or more authorised models for strategy, copy, research assistance, image creation, video, audio, translation, layout, presentations and channel adaptation.

AI creates suggestions. SecurePay authorises what may enter the market.

No model output becomes distributable merely because a model produced it.

## 5. Provenance and approval principle

Every market asset must be traceable to:

- campaign / market objective
- source doctrine / approved source material
- relevant pricing or product snapshot when claims depend on it
- models used internally
- human editor / owner
- Brand Guardian decision
- Compliance / Legal decision when required
- approval role / actor
- asset version
- market status: DRAFT, REVIEW, APPROVED_FOR_MARKET, RETIRED

If a governing source changes, affected assets must be identifiable for review or retirement.

## 6. Plug Market Kit principle

Plugs receive only CURRENT authorised material appropriate to their active market standing and qualifications.

Allowed personalisation may include name, KS Number, approved qualification, contact information and territory/context where policy permits.

Locked SecurePay claims, legal wording, prices, logos and required disclaimers cannot be rewritten by the Plug.

Sharing a marketing asset or being selected from an opportunity does not create a financial entitlement.

## 7. Agreement-level 10% rule

The Lifetime 10% agreement share is created only from explicit agreement attribution.

> No Plug KS Number written into the agreement = no agreement 10% share.

The share must not be inferred from:

- who shared a link
- who created marketing material
- who expressed interest in an opportunity
- who was selected by a customer
- Community activity
- cookies or tracking
- historical referral relationship alone

Authority chain:

Agreement contains validated Plug KS Number → agreement attribution locks → qualifying platform fee is confirmed by SecurePay → SecurePay calculates 10% → Outreach displays confirmed private share truth.

The separate month-10 subscription retention reward remains a distinct policy/domain unless separately changed.

# Completion roadmap — 7 major phases

## Phase 1 — Creative Studio + Model Registry

### Goal
Create the world-class production room for authorised SecurePay staff.

### Deliverables
- first-class `/studio` staff route aligned to Lovable visual language
- campaign brief / intent input
- Studio job/workspace model
- Model Registry with capability categories and provider status
- explicit model/provider selection for authorised users
- automatic capability recommendation without pretending the router is infallible
- production lanes for copy, image, video/audio concept, presentation/document and channel adaptation
- generated-output status remains DRAFT
- human edit/revision/version history foundation
- source references attached to Studio jobs
- no live ad execution in this phase

### Acceptance gate
An authorised staff user can take a campaign objective, open Studio, see which approved model capabilities fit the job, create/revise draft variants and keep every output non-distributable until approval.

## Phase 2 — Brand, Claims, Legal/Compliance Approval + Provenance

### Goal
Ensure every public SecurePay statement is traceable, current and authorised.

### Deliverables
- Claims Registry / approved-source references
- Brand Guardian expansion
- approval policy engine by content/risk class
- Brand review
- Compliance review where required
- Legal review where required
- review comments / requested changes
- APPROVED_FOR_MARKET and RETIRED states
- immutable approval/provenance events
- source-version impact detection foundation
- publication blocked unless approval conditions are satisfied

### Acceptance gate
No asset can enter a distribution or Plug Market Kit release path without the exact required approvals and a provenance record.

## Phase 3 — Authoritative Asset Library + Plug Market Kit

### Goal
Create one source of truth for current market material and give Plugs a clean authorised cupboard.

### Deliverables
- staff Asset Library
- searchable/filterable asset types and campaign packs
- status/version/expiry/currentness
- `Release to Plugs` publication action
- Market Kit route for ACTIVE Plugs
- capability/qualification-aware packs
- controlled personalisation zones
- QR/share/print-ready metadata foundation
- retirement/replacement handling
- no private campaign strategy or internal legal discussion exposed to Plugs

### Acceptance gate
An ACTIVE qualified Plug can find, personalise only permitted fields and use a CURRENT approved asset; retired/unapproved material cannot be presented as authorised.

## Phase 4 — Distribution Command + Budget Authority + Provider Adapters

### Goal
Move Outreach from simulated reach planning toward safe, authorised execution.

### Deliverables
- channel registry / provider readiness
- Google Ads adapter boundary
- Meta adapter boundary
- approved WhatsApp/business messaging boundary where available
- email/SMS/owned social/partner channel boundaries
- future-channel plug-in contract
- campaign/channel budget proposal
- budget approval and limits
- daily/total spend caps
- start/end windows
- execution role separation
- pause / kill switch
- immutable execution request/status evidence
- reconciliation hooks
- no model or designer receives spending authority merely by creating an asset

### Acceptance gate
Only an APPROVED_FOR_MARKET asset with authorised distribution plan, approved budget and allowed provider can be queued/executed. No unchecked direct AI-to-ad-spend path exists.

## Phase 5 — Engagement, Impact, Intelligence + Growth Director Closed Loop

### Goal
Turn market response into the next commercial decision.

### Deliverables
- engagement events from supported channels
- outcome-first impact projections
- campaign/asset/channel learning
- market insight intake from Plugs and staff with privacy controls
- feedback from Market Kit usage
- attribution that does not manufacture financial entitlement
- Growth Director recommendations grounded in evidence
- recommendation confidence/source display
- `What happened → what it means → what can I do next`
- rapid-response campaign path for misinformation/confusion/market opportunities

### Acceptance gate
The system can explain what reached the market, what happened, what was learned and what action it recommends next without presenting vanity metrics as business truth.

## Phase 6 — Complete Market Network Commercial Journey

### Goal
Finish the human relationship path while preserving authority separation.

### SecurePay backend dependencies
Close/review/merge in order before building further stacked production authority:

1. PR #152 — Market Readiness
2. PR #153 — Plug Market Entry
3. PR #154 — Opportunity Offer/Interest
4. PR #155 — Customer Market Request

Do not keep indefinitely stacking new SecurePay PRs ahead of this review chain.

### New authority slices after the chain is integrated
- requester can view privacy-safe interested Plug candidates
- requester selection authority
- customer/Plug relationship authority
- relationship does not imply referral/share entitlement
- contact exchange only after explicit authorised relationship state
- Master/territory logic remains separate unless deliberately introduced

### Acceptance gate
Customer asks for help → qualified ACTIVE Plug expresses interest → customer selects → relationship is recorded, with no automatic money/referral entitlement.

## Phase 7 — Agreement Plug Attribution + Lifetime 10% + Production Closure

### Goal
Complete the commercial promise and prove the full system end-to-end.

### Deliverables
- optional agreement field: Plug KS Number / `Who helped you get this agreement going?`
- validate KS Number and allowed Plug attribution state
- snapshot attribution into agreement
- attribution lock at the defined agreement lock point
- no retroactive addition after lock
- no attribution inference from opportunity/referral/link sharing
- qualifying platform-fee event authority
- immutable 10% entitlement evidence from qualifying platform fee
- private My Market projection: pending/earned/paid as authorised by backend truth
- no Outreach-side calculation of entitlement
- negative tests for missing/invalid/late Plug KS Number
- final SecurePay/Outreach integration
- production environment/configuration review
- role/privacy/security tests
- full staff, Plug and customer browser journeys
- distribution safety tests
- approval/provenance tests
- deployment runbook and launch checklist

### Acceptance gate
The complete tested journey works:

Staff intelligence → campaign → Studio → approvals → distribution/Market Kit → customer market request → qualified Plug → selection → relationship → agreement → explicit Plug KS Number → qualifying platform fee → backend 10% evidence → private My Market display → Engagement/Impact feeds learning back to Outreach.

And the critical negative truth works:

> If the Plug KS Number is not in the agreement, no agreement 10% entitlement exists.

# Execution discipline

## Branch/PR rule
Each phase is implemented in phase-scoped Outreach PRs. Merge only when the repository's migration/seed/lint/typecheck/tests/production-build validation is green.

SecurePayAPI follows its repository policy: human review/merge gate. Do not auto-merge SecurePay authority PRs when repository policy forbids it.

## Visual rule
Before each major UI phase, inspect the current Lovable reference. Do not drift into a generic admin dashboard. Use human-language navigation and progressive disclosure.

## Authority rule
UI never invents backend truth. When an authority is unavailable, fail closed or clearly label a demo/prototype path.

## Privacy rule
Customer PII, Circle/private conversation and internal legal/compliance discussion do not leak into market-visible feeds or Plug materials.

## AI rule
No AI provider has intrinsic publication, budget, legal or financial authority. Models are tools inside governed workflows.

## Distribution rule
Never enable autonomous unrestricted spend or bulk distribution merely because creative generation succeeded.

## Completion definition
The programme is complete only when all seven acceptance gates are met and the final E2E launch checklist passes. A beautiful prototype alone is not completion; backend-only authority alone is not completion.
